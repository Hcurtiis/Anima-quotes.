## Removed from this feed

Death Note's justice/chip/interesting lines, Evangelion's ceiling, Sailor Moon attack lines, the Jiji jokes, Trigun's requests line, Cowboy Bebop's hunger/alive lines, the Gurren Lagann challenge, FMA's rain/one-is-all lines, JoJo's refusal and Saitama's introduction were removed for focus, not declared false.

The Haku line was removed because it is more plot-specific than motivational. The All Might lines and Eren fight line were held out of this audit because an accessible, sufficiently specific source was not established in this pass. No replacement motivational wording was invented and attributed to those characters.

The public feed is maintained in Hcurtiis/Anima-quotes. (including the final dot). The copy in Anima-ios is a snapshot; update both when curating. Scriptable downloads the public copy after its six-hour cache expires.
